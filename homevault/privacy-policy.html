HomeVault Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

English বাংলা

HomeVault: Warranty Tracker ("HomeVault", "the app") is a home inventory and warranty tracker for Android, published by Md Imran Hossain ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app. A Bangla version follows the English one.

The short version

  • HomeVault works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your information anywhere.
  • We never receive, see, sell or share anything you enter.
  • Information leaves your phone only when you choose to send it: when you share an export, save a backup file, or when Android's own backup (if you turned it on) copies app data to your Google account.

What HomeVault stores, and where

Everything below is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Item details you type: name, category, brand, model, serial number, store, purchase date, price, warranty length and notes.
  • Photos: a photo of each item and a photo of its receipt or invoice, if you add them.
  • Report details (optional): an owner name, home address and insurance policy number, if you fill them in on the Reports tab. They are used only to print on the cover of the PDF report you create.
  • Settings: theme, currency symbol, sounds on or off, and language.
  • Reminder list: each item's number, name and warranty end date, kept so that reminders can be shown even when the app is closed.

What HomeVault does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The Android permission for internet access is removed from the app.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, phone number or any account.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and does not use an advertising ID.

Camera and photos

  • Taking a photo: HomeVault opens your phone's camera app, which saves the picture directly into HomeVault's private storage. HomeVault does not have camera permission. Some camera apps may also keep their own copy in your gallery; that depends on the camera app's settings.
  • Choosing an existing image: HomeVault uses the Android photo picker, which gives the app only the image you select. The image is copied into HomeVault's private storage; your original is not changed. The app cannot browse the rest of your photo library.

Notifications

With your permission, HomeVault shows reminders 30 days, 7 days and 1 day before a warranty ends, and on the last day. The reminders are scheduled and shown on your phone; nothing is sent to us or to any notification service. The app also asks Android to restart the reminder schedule after your phone restarts. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Exports and sharing

When you tap Export, HomeVault creates a PDF report or a CSV file on your phone and opens Android's share menu. The file goes only where you send it, for example email, a messaging app or cloud storage, and that service's own privacy policy then applies. A PDF report can include your item photos, receipts and the optional owner name, address and policy number. The app keeps a temporary copy of the file in its private cache, which Android may clear and which is removed when you uninstall the app.

Backup files you create

When you choose "Back up to file" in Settings, HomeVault saves one file containing your items, photos, receipts and settings to the place you pick, such as your phone's storage, a memory card or a cloud folder. The file is not password-protected, so keep it somewhere safe. When you choose "Restore from file", HomeVault reads only the file you select and replaces the information in the app with what the file contains. We never receive your backup files.

Android backup and moving to a new phone

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy HomeVault's item details, settings and reminder list to your personal Google account backup, and restore them when you set up a new phone. Item and receipt photos are not included in that online backup. During a direct transfer from one phone to another, Android copies the photos too. This is done by Android and your Google account under Google's terms; we cannot see or access it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Security

Your information is protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps one app's private storage away from other apps, and by your phone's screen lock and storage encryption. HomeVault does not add its own password or encryption, so anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app. Exported files and backup files are only as safe as the place you store or send them.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays in the app until you delete it. Deleting an item also deletes its photo and receipt from the app. Uninstalling HomeVault, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes everything the app stored on your phone. Files you exported or backed up are not deleted by the app; delete them where you saved or sent them. Because we hold no copy of your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

If you email us

If you contact us at mdimranhossain.ist@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you, do not share it, and delete the conversation once your question is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Children

HomeVault is made for adults managing a household and is not directed at children. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

You can see, change, export and delete all of your information inside the app at any time. Since we do not hold any of it, we cannot access, correct or delete it for you, but we are happy to answer questions about how the app handles it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a new version of the app adds a feature that handles information differently, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date before the change takes effect.

তথ্য রাখা ও মুছে ফেলা
